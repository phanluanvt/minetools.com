Minecraft Nether Portal Calculator

Convert X and Z coordinates between the Overworld and Nether using Minecraft's 8:1 horizontal coordinate ratio.

Nether: X 100, Z -50Y coordinate is not converted by the 8:1 rule.

Overworld to Nether coordinates

For horizontal travel, divide Overworld X and Z by 8 to estimate the corresponding Nether coordinates. Going from the Nether to the Overworld, multiply X and Z by 8. Portal linking can also depend on nearby portals and placement, so use this as the coordinate starting point.
